Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · applies to the Android release of Capybara Flow: Color Shooter.

This policy explains what information the game processes, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short because the game is simple: there is no account and no sign-up.

Capybara Flow: Color Shooter (“the Game”) is developed and published by pandab (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, contact pandabproduction2020@gmail.com.

Advertising and your choices

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Depending on your region, age group and settings, ads may be personalized using your advertising ID. Players under 13, or who choose not to say, receive non-personalized ads only. You can control this at any time:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Opt out of personalized ads: turn off “Personalized ads” in the same screen.
  • Remove ads entirely: buy the “Remove Ads” product in the Game.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA or UK), a consent prompt is shown before ads are requested. If you decline personalized ads, the Game continues with non-personalized ads or no ads, and analytics collection is disabled. You can change your choice later from Settings → About → Privacy Policy or your device ad settings.

Children

The Game is intended for a mixed audience, including children and older players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. On first launch, a neutral age screen lets players choose an age group. Players under 13, or who choose not to say, are treated as children for advertising purposes: ads are non-personalized, ad content is limited to a G rating, and analytics collection is disabled. If you believe a child has provided information to us, email us and we will delete it. If you are a parent and see an ad that seems inappropriate, please report it to us.

Data retention

  • Local game data remains on your device until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall it.
  • Analytics data is retained by Google/Firebase according to the retention period configured for the project (currently 14 months).
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Purchase records are retained by Google as required for tax and accounting law.

Security

Data sent by the Game's third-party SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we choose reputable providers and limit what is collected to what the Game actually needs.

International transfers

Google processes data in data centers around the world. Where data leaves your country, Google relies on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any right, email pandabproduction2020@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the Game or on the store listing before they take effect.

Data & deletion

How to see, reset or delete what the Game stores.

Delete data stored on your device

The Game keeps your progress and settings locally. To remove them:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Capybara Flow: Color Shooter.
  2. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage (this resets progress).
  3. Or uninstall the Game, which removes all local data.

Clearing local data cannot be undone. Purchases remain tied to your Google account and can be restored in the Game.

Request deletion of analytics or support data

Email pandabproduction2020@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Include your device model and, if possible, your approximate install date so we can locate the relevant records. We will confirm and delete what we control within 30 days. Data held by Google (Firebase, AdMob, Play Billing) is handled under Google's own retention rules; we will forward your request where required.

What is deleted: analytics events associated with your app instance and any support emails we hold.
What may remain: aggregated, non-identifying statistics, and purchase/tax records that Google must keep by law.
