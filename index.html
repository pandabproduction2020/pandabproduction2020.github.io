About pandab

pandab is a small independent studio making casual games for Android. We believe a good mobile game fits into the gaps of your day: easy to pick up, kind to your battery, and honest about your data.

Every game we ship is playable without an account, is safe for a mixed audience, and spells out plainly what it collects — see each game's policy page for the details.
